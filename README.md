# Electric-Vehicles-Analysis-In-India

## OVERVIEW

Analysis of electric vehicle (EV) sales in India from 2022 to 2024.

Focused on understanding trends, growth patterns, and seasonal analysis.

To highlight state-wise and maker-wise contributions to EV growth.

## KEY INSIGHTS

2-wheelers dominate the sales volume, aligning with consumer preferences for affordability and utility.

A significant upward trend in EV sales from 2022 to 2024.

Ola Electric(2-wheeler) and Tata Motors(4-wheeler) are the top-performing manufacturers.

TVS and Ather are next in line, contributing significantly to sales and market growth.

Electric vehicle sales are growing at a rapid rate of 93.91%, signaling a strong future for EV adoption.

Goa (9.84%), Karnataka (7.84%), and Delhi (6.76%) lead in EV penetration rates.

States like Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, and Meghalaya show the lowest penetration rates, representing untapped markets.

The combined sales of 2-wheelers and 4-wheelers exhibit consistent growth, suggesting increasing consumer adoption.

Peak sales are observed during March, likely influenced by year-end incentives(FAME 2 Subsidy ending month) and new fiscal year launches.

## RECOMMENDATIONS

Encourage EV adoption in low-penetration states by offering subsidies and incentives.

Enhance charging infrastructure in Tier-2 cities to support growing EV demand.

Launch promotional campaigns during peak sales months (March) to maximize revenue.

Introduce special discounts and offers during slower monsoon months to stabilize sales.

Invest in affordable 2-wheeler EV models.

Target states with lower penetration to capture untapped markets.
